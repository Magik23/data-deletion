Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request deletion of their personal data collected through our services.

How to Request Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their data by contacting us via WhatsApp or email. All requests will be processed within a reasonable timeframe.

Contact Information

To request deletion of your data, please contact us:

  • Email: your-email@example.com
  • WhatsApp: Your business WhatsApp number

What Happens After a Request

Once a deletion request is received, we will:

  • Verify the identity of the requester when necessary
  • Delete relevant personal data from our systems
  • Confirm completion of the request

Data We May Store

We may store limited data such as:

  • Phone number used for communication
  • Messages exchanged via WhatsApp
  • Basic interaction metadata required for service operation

Third-Party Services

Our system may use third-party services such as:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API)
  • Make (automation platform)
  • OpenAI (AI processing)

Each provider processes data according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

If you have any questions about data deletion, contact us via WhatsApp or email.
